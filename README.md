Ember Court is an original card duel that plays offline in a desktop browser from static files. You lead one of four courts against the Warden: play creatures and spells with mana that grows each turn, send creatures across the table, and reduce the Warden's life from 20 to 0. Creatures carry Guard, Charge and Drain; suits are told apart by colour and shape as well as by name.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Mouse and keyboard both work: click a card to play it, click one of your creatures then an enemy to attack, or use keys 1–9, E to end the turn, and Esc to cancel.

## Hand it in

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
