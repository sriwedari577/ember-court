EMBER COURT

A duel of four courts. Reduce the Warden's life from 20 to 0 before yours runs out. Each turn you gain mana, draw a card, play creatures and spells, and send your creatures across the table.

▲ Ember burns  ● Tide heals and drains  ✦ Grove guards  ◆ Stone endures

Creatures cost mana (blue number). A creature cannot attack the turn it is played, unless it has Charge. Guard creatures must be attacked before anything else. Drain creatures heal you for the damage they deal.

Spells take effect at once: damage, healing, drawing cards, or fortifying a creature. When your deck runs out, drawing hurts instead — fatigue.

To attack: click one of your ready creatures, then click an enemy creature or the Warden. Both creatures strike each other.

VICTORY

The Warden's court falls silent. The ember is yours.

DEFEAT

The Warden keeps the court — this time.
